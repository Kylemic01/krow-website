Before you book

The questions we keep getting.

What's actually different about Krow vs. other med spa consultants?

Every consultant we compete with sells advice: a plan, a framework, a binder you're left to implement. Krow builds the systems and runs them with you. If a system isn't working, we rebuild it. That's a different kind of engagement than strategy alone.

Do I have to commit to the Scale Package to get the Sprint?

No. The Sprint is designed to stand alone, and you keep the written plan and the quick-win either way. Most clients who go through it choose to implement what the plan identifies, but nothing obligates you to.

Can I upgrade later if I start with the Sprint?

Yes. Contact us and we'll talk through how a Sprint engagement rolls into a Scale Package if you decide to move forward.

Does this replace our current marketing agency?

Usually not, and we don't manage ad spend or media buying. We look at what happens after the lead comes in: the follow-up, the no-shows, the lapsed patients, the speed to respond. Where marketing creative is part of the engagement, it's something your own team or agency runs. We're not taking over your ad accounts.

What happens after the Scale Package or Accelerator ends?

The systems keep running on a month-to-month retainer. The day the retainer stops is the day the leak it's plugging comes back. That's the honest reason clients continue, not a lock-in. Contact us for current retainer details.

How does the guarantee actually work?

Each tier carries its own: the Sprint refunds in full if we don't find more in recoverable revenue than the fee. The Scale Package gets audited, rebuilt, and extended at no additional cost if it isn't showing measurable improvement. The Accelerator is a full refund if we don't document $100,000 in added revenue.

What does this cost?

It depends on scope, and we'd rather talk it through than post a number that may not fit your practice. Contact us and we'll walk through which tier makes sense and what it costs for you.
