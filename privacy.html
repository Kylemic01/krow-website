Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026  •  Effective immediately

Krow Med Spa Growth ("Krow," "we," "us," or "our") operates heykrow.com and the related webinar funnel. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights regarding that information.

By using our website, registering for our webinar, or submitting your information through any of our forms, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Full name, email address, and mobile phone number (collected at registration)
  • Practice revenue range, challenge type, years in business, and number of providers (collected via pre-webinar survey)
  • Open-text responses about your practice goals and challenges (collected via optional follow-up survey)
  • Payment information (processed directly by Stripe — we do not store card details)

Information collected automatically:

  • Pages visited, links clicked, and time spent on our site (via Meta Pixel and standard server logs)
  • Browser type, device type, IP address, and referring URL
  • Cookie identifiers placed by Meta (Facebook) for advertising purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm your webinar registration and send access details
  • To send email and SMS reminders before the session
  • To personalize the content and benchmarks shown during the webinar based on your survey answers
  • To follow up after the webinar with relevant offers and resources
  • To process payments for any purchased services via Stripe
  • To run targeted advertising on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) using aggregated audience data
  • To analyze and improve our content, funnel, and services

3. SMS and Email Communications

By checking the consent box on our registration form, you agree to receive marketing and informational messages from Krow Med Spa Growth via email and SMS text message. This includes webinar reminders, follow-up sequences, and promotional messages related to our services.

SMS: Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. To opt out of SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message we send. For help, reply HELP or contact us at the email below.

Email: Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time.

Opting out of marketing communications does not affect transactional messages (e.g., receipts, service-related notices).

4. Third-Party Services We Use

We share data with the following services to operate our business:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe handles all card data under its own privacy policy.
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — advertising. We use the Meta Pixel to measure ad performance and build custom audiences. Meta may use this data under its own data policy.
  • MailerLite — email delivery and list management.
  • n8n — workflow automation that routes registration data between our systems.
  • Zoom / Demio — webinar hosting. Your name and email may be passed to the webinar platform upon registration.
  • Calendly — appointment scheduling for strategy calls.
  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies, including the Meta Pixel, to understand how visitors interact with our site and to serve relevant ads on Meta platforms. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. If you request deletion of your data, we will remove it from our active systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below.
